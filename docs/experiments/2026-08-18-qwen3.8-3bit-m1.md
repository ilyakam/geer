# Qwen3.8 3-bit profiles on a 16 GB M1

Experiment period: 2026-08-18 through 2026-08-21.
Outcome: M4 checks passed in limited configurations; physical-M1 loads failed.
Disposition: experimental profiles retired; no 16 GB support established.

## Question and scope

Could a full or text-only 3-bit Qwen3.8-27B artifact support Geer's coding
stack on a 2020 Apple M1 MacBook Pro with 16 GB unified memory?

## Configuration

- Full VLM: `lukaskremla/Qwen3.8-27B-3bit-MLX`, revision
  `b3503b31b36125107e12a2b57fbe690c37bfc053`, 12,719,813,675 bytes.
- Text-only: `lukaskremla/Qwen3.8-27B-3bit-MLX-TextOnly`, revision
  `c98bba5926f51fec1c8d8737e577221673f524d7`, 11,791,570,738 bytes.
- Serving stack: oMLX 0.5.3 at
  `31d07a7fdbbb499d4520188fb7e8f7bdbfe4c770`, MLX 0.32.0 and
  MLX-VLM 0.6.3. Serving and external conversion versions were distinct.
- Compatibility and capped-memory host: M4 Max, 128 GB unified memory.
  Destination host: physical Apple M1, 16 GB. The text-only experimental
  profile used 8K context, 4-bit TurboQuant KV, and a 13 GiB model guard.

## Result

The full VLM passed short text, tool, image, and endpoint checks on the M4.
On the physical M1, it stopped before loading: the estimated footprint was
12.41 GB against a 7.69 GB guard ceiling. Those are the runtime's reported
units, not measurements of a successfully resident model.

On the M4, a text-only run with a 12 GiB guard crossed the 11.4 GB prefill
watermark. At 13 GiB, 4K and 8K requests passed, while 16K crossed the
watermark. These were memory-capped tests on the larger host.

The physical M1 text-only request also failed before loading, with an
estimated footprint of 11.51 GB against a 10.67 GB ceiling. A later manual
Metal-limit adjustment changed the reported cap to 13.0 GB. No completed
generation result after that adjustment was retained. It does not establish
recovery, sustained memory safety, or usable coding performance.

Short throughput figures in the retained history lack sufficiently matched
prompt and completion sizes for a comparative benchmark, so none is reported.

## Decision and revisit condition

Retain the failed physical-host results. A cap change alone is insufficient
reason to repeat the complete stack. First qualify the runtime's effective
limits and recovery behavior, then run one isolated bounded generation on
the physical host while measuring memory pressure, swap, and latency.
Only after that succeeds should cold/warm, context, Pi/T3, retrieval, and
practical edit/test checks proceed.

These external artifacts' exact provenance must be reviewed before reuse.
Their snapshot pins and the official model's license link are identifiers,
not a claim that Geer independently reproduced their conversion process.
